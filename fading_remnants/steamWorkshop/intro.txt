[h2]Mod Overview[/h2]
This mod automatically removes redundant entities, including:
- Opened chests
- Broken Blood Donation Machines
- Broken Fortune Telling Machines
- Broken Slot Machines
- Broken Restock Machines
and more.

I created this mod because piles of opened chests spawned by the D20 are quite annoying, along with leftover item pedestals generated by Genesis, D6 and Void items.

[h3]Customization[/h3]
If you install [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3701683951]Steam Workshop - Mod Config Menu[/url], you can fully tweak the fade-out animation settings of this mod.
